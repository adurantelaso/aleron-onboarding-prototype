# Aleron MD · Patient onboarding (prototype)

A clickable prototype of Aleron MD patient onboarding. It starts on a condensed aleronmd.com homepage and follows a new member through purchase, activating their account, booking the initial consultation with their physician (User Story 0), and the information they give before that visit (User Story 1).

**Live:** https://adurantelaso.github.io/aleron-onboarding-prototype/

Everything here is fictional. The member, the physician and the openings are demo data, and nothing is sent anywhere: payment, email, texts, device connections and record requests are simulated. Your clicks are saved only in your own browser; **Demo → Reset demo** (bottom left) starts over, and **Demo** also jumps to any step of either story.

This repository holds only the built site (published with GitHub Pages). The source lives in the `patient-onboarding` app of the orders-and-notes project.
